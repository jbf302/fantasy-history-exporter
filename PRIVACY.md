# Privacy

Fantasy History Exporter for Yahoo runs entirely in your browser.

- **What it reads:** your Yahoo Fantasy Football league's data (standings, drafts, matchups, lineups, team and manager names), using the Yahoo session you're already signed in with.
- **Where it goes:** only into a JSON file that you save to your own computer. Nothing is sent to the author or to any third party. There is no server, analytics, or tracking.
- **Network requests:** only to Yahoo Fantasy hosts (`football.fantasysports.yahoo.com`, `fantasysports.yahooapis.com`, `pub-api-ro.fantasysports.yahoo.com`, `pub-api-rw.fantasysports.yahoo.com`).
- **Credentials:** the extension never sees or stores your Yahoo password, and does not read your cookies. Requests are made from your browser, which attaches your session itself.
- **Storage:** the extension doesn't persist anything. The export exists in memory until you save it or close the panel.

The exported file contains the names Yahoo shows for your league's managers and teams. Treat it like any league data: be thoughtful before posting it publicly.
